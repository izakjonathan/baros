# Research matrix

## Workforce scheduling

- Planday: availability, reusable templates, open shifts, shift requests, swaps/handover, mobile employee schedule and clocking.
- Deputy: multi-location scheduling, timekeeping, compliance, communication, timesheet approval and payroll export.
- 7shifts: restaurant-specific scheduling, time clocking, labour compliance, manager log book, tasks, engagement, tips, payroll.
- When I Work: scheduling, time clock, attendance notices, custom schedule and timesheet exports.
- Homebase: copy/templates, labour cost visibility, schedule notifications, availability, swaps, time clock, payroll flow.
- Connecteam: scheduling, time clock, jobs, forms/checklists, tasks, knowledge, communications, onboarding.

## Restaurant back office

- MarketMan: inventory control, purchasing, expense tracking, supplier/order management, multi-unit operations, recipe costing and price-linked food cost.
- Restaurant365: connected scheduling, forecasting, attendance, payroll, accounting, inventory and financial reporting; scheduled-versus-actual labour and POS/vendor integrations.

## Key conclusion

Bar Ops should not clone one competitor. Its advantage is connecting a strong hourly-workforce system with bar-specific stock, procurement, checklists, equipment, events, and manager handover in one coherent operational model.
