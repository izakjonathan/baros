# Employee Workspace Status

The employee workspace is functionally implemented but **not yet redesigned**.

Previous release notes describing the employee workspace redesign as complete were inaccurate.

## Current status

- Employee authentication and linked-profile access exist.
- Managers may intentionally enter the employee workspace when linked to an employee profile.
- Employee data access must remain scoped to that linked employee identity.
- Accessibility and containment foundations have been applied in places.
- The complete visual and interaction redesign remains pending.

## Future dedicated redesign scope

- Employee Home
- My Shifts
- Open Shifts
- Hours and clock state
- Availability
- Requests
- Notifications
- More sheet and mobile navigation
- Employee forms, dialogs, loading, empty, and error states

The redesign must not be mixed into authorization, data-integrity, or architecture remediation releases.
